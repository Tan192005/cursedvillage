How to Play & Win (Step-by-Step)

Updated hardcore survival horror loop built into your Unity 6 project.

Step 1 • Explore the 4 Quadrants

Find the 4 Scattered Totems

No yellow guide lines or far-away sky beams—explore the dark village and forest using your wide flashlight and radar. A Cursed Totem's label only reveals itself when you are within 7 meters!

Locations: NW Blacksmith • SW Forest Cabin • SE River Woods • NE Deep Woods
Step 2 • Hold [R] (Long Press)

2-Second Ritual Burn Activation

Stand close to a Cursed Totem and Hold [R] for 2.0 seconds. The totem shakes and levitates while the charging bar fills to 100%, then explodes into orange purifying flames!

Long Press [R]: 2.0s Charging Bar + Ritual Levitation
Step 3 • Watch the Warning

Zombies Warn Before Attacking

When a zombie spots you (28m), it stops, turns, and growls with a 2.5s ⚠ ATTACKING IN… countdown before it charges. Their HP bars only appear when they are close (≤ 8m). Use your 4 melee weapons to fight back!

2.5s Warning • 0.7s Wind-up • 1.2s Attack Cooldown
Step 4 • Follow the Radar Signal

Escape Through the Hidden Portal

Burning all 4 / 4 Totems unlocks a Hidden Exit Portal in one of the deep forest corners. Follow the Hot / Cold Radar Signal at the top of your screen to find it and trigger 🏆 GAME SUCCESS!

Radar Signal: ❄ COLD → ♨ WARM → 🔥 HOT → ★ PORTAL VISIBLE!
